Frequently Asked Questions

What is the estimated delivery time for my order?

We strive to deliver all orders within 3-5 business days. During holidays, it might take a day or two extra.

Which payment methods do you currently accept?

Currently, we support Cash on Delivery (COD) and direct Bank Transfers for pre-paid orders.

We offer a hassle-free 7-day return policy. Simply email us a photo of the product, and we will arrange a replacement.

Our quality control team manually inspects every batch for size, texture, and taste standards.

Is my personal data safe?

Absolutely. We use industry-standard encryption to protect your personal and payment information.
